# Can the same five fixed passenger duties finish with a different set of charging visits?

Recheck the 12 October cells for which PARX at 60 kW made the price-specific, previously selected station visits infeasible, including with the plug and terminal rows removed. For each target select exactly one predetermined feasible donor: the flat-price sibling in the same campaign family with identical input instance, physical assumptions, required return energy, and five GIRO passenger sequences. Treat missing or ambiguous donors as unverified. Do not optimize by choosing the best donor. Only the electricity-price input and the chosen visits may differ. Verify the timetable and directed travel on the receiver's source graph, exactly-once passenger duties, energy after every movement, the terminal requirement and plug occupancy at each minute. Use one constant delivery rate within each stored positive session to reconstruct a feasible minute profile. Report a feasible cost upper bound and retain all misses. This measures feasibility of alternative station visits on the historical continuous-energy model, with 240 kWh batteries, 350 kW opportunity chargers, zero reserve and the original aggregate terminal target. The experiment does not optimize over stations or validate raw transfer aliases or full GIRO physics.
