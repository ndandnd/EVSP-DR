# RAW k40 MIP campaign

This campaign tests integer assembly from independently generated exact-CG
columns for the full 947-trip k40 instance. It does **not** add or warm-start
from GIRO duties.

The four inputs are the 24-hour snapshots from two independent factorial
campaigns:

- `R1_CA`, `R2_CA`: cover master initialized with artificials only;
- `R1_CS`, `R2_CS`: cover master initialized with feasible singleton routes.

All four MIPs use strict exact-once partitioning, two-stage fleet-first
optimization, eight threads, an eight-hour solver budget, and MIP gap
`0.0001`. They run on Scaglione with `--no-requeue`. The MIP callback records
incumbent/bound checkpoints at 0, 5, 15, 30, and 60 minutes and then hourly.

The preparation job validates the frozen pool and journal bytes, the 947-trip
instance and flat tariff hashes, source treatments, reviewed Git commit,
Python/Gurobi identity, absence of an external partition start, and all four
job specifications before submitting any MIP.

`CA` is the closest available saved-pool test to an empty start: the real
columns were generated by pricing from an artificial master. `CS` is also
non-GIRO, but its pool includes feasible singleton seed routes. Neither arm is
a from-scratch column-generation rerun; both solve the integer master over
their frozen 24-hour column journals.
