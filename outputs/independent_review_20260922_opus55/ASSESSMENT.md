# Audit of the independent review

The review is useful for checking the proposed paper's scope and suggesting controls. It is not a reliable standalone account of the latest research state. Its original text is preserved in [REVIEW.md](REVIEW.md); reviewer finding numbers below are prefixed **Opus** to distinguish them from the earlier independent review's F numbers. No solver was run for this audit.

| Review statement | Audit outcome | Evidence or correction |
|---|---|---|
| Figure 1 has 24/24 sequential and 6/24 fresh target matches. | Verified | Recounted directly from the figure CSV; see audit_counts.json. |
| Sequential growth uses complete GIRO duties. | Verified; add explicitly to the figure caption | Trip grouping is informed by the reference schedule. This does not mean injecting GIRO route columns. A grouping-free control remains useful. |
| The sequential advantage does not scale at k33–35. | Not established by this comparison | The observed larger sequential runs miss their targets under their budgets. There is no matched fresh comparison here to determine whether the relative advantage disappears. |
| Fresh uses only 3–30% of its allowance. | Refuted as a range for Figure 1 | Actual range is 2.77–56.16%. C4 k5 uses 11.326 minutes of 20.167 allowed; C6 k5 uses 40.84%. The broader statement that every fresh run certifies before its allowance is exhausted remains true. |
| Three of four original dives reached eight buses. | Refuted as written | All four timing.json files record an eight-bus dive incumbent. Three original final MIPs retained/recovered eight; C1's final MIP did not receive its eight-bus incumbent. These are different stages. |
| Current integer-directed evidence is only the original four-case, one-seed pilot. | Incomplete | The later matched replication has 7/8 treatments versus 0/8 controls on four cases at two seeds. It remains a selected small sample. One k15 follow-up reached 15 versus control17; other cases did not show that success. |
| Baseline Figures 1–4 do not establish full GIRO operational or demand-response performance. | Verified within that scope | Their baseline is flat-tariff, zero reserve, 240 kW PARX, no terminal floor or shared capacity. Separate stricter-physics and tariff experiments exist and must not be described as absent from the project. |
| A peak-concurrency bound equal to k identifies a known fleet optimum. | Conditional | It proves a lower bound. A feasible k-bus schedule under the same event model is still needed for the upper bound. A GIRO schedule alone does not prove event-lattice feasibility under different assumptions. |
| The timing contrasts need hardware/variation disclosure. | Supported qualification | Preserve the measured paired effects, disclose three cases with two order repetitions and cross-allocation variation. This does not prove that the effects are entirely noise. |
| Packed speedup combines compact storage with fewer arcs and a different scan. | Verified; already explained in the reader's guide | 3,319,685 explicit arcs versus1,180,257 retained packed arcs; five fixed-dual checks on one26-trip input. Do not attribute the full speedup to storage alone. |
| The price-and-branch limitation itself is the paper's novelty. | Not a claim to adopt | The contribution would need to be a method, analysis or application result beyond this known general issue. A literature audit is required before making a novelty claim in either direction. |

The [bound audit](audit_bounds.md) rejects the proposed numerical floors until per-instance cost envelopes and matching pricing records are checked. No new fleet certificate may be generated by substituting an assumed per-route cost bound or treating missing pricing evidence as zero reduced cost.

The [long-wait audit](audit_long_wait.md) verifies a modeling restriction in three source pins using 12 solver-free fixtures: direct connections are limited to 57-minute gaps, and station bridges require positive charging. A top-grid-SOC bus therefore lacks a zero-charge bridge for a longer gap, although ordinary shorter waits remain possible. This is documented historical graph pruning, not a new packed-graph regression. Certificates remain scoped to that restricted graph; whether this restriction changes production fleets or charging costs is unresolved.

## Actions justified now

Clarify duty-informed grouping in Figure 1; preserve all reported endpoints. Prepare a single-factor long-idle-connection comparison before changing the production model. Add timing-variation and combined-implementation qualifications to Figures 4–5. Keep the original review separate from verified results. Before a new experimental campaign, develop a matched grouping-free control and a correctly certified, instance-specific fleet-target rule; do not relabel an RMP route weight as a fleet lower bound.
