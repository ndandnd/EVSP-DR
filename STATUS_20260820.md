## 3. Findings

**F1 — The ladder exists.** 22 PREFLIGHT, 21 SEED, 23 primary CG, 30 sensitivity
CG. Artificials hit zero at iteration 1 in every cell (the singleton pool covers
all trips), so artificial elimination was never a real issue.

**F2 — Exactness at small scale is established.** All nine k2/k3/k5 primary-grid
cells are pricing-certified in 8 s to 86 min. First censoring appears at **k8**
(2 of 3 replicates) — a direct answer to the handoff's named question.

**F3 — `max_iters` was the binding constraint, not runtime (`B0019`).** The
frozen ladder never passes `--max-iters`, so every cell inherited the argparse
default of 2000. `k08_s1` stopped at 3.4 h of a 6 h budget; `k20_s1` at 6.1 h of
12 h. Roughly half the allocated compute was discarded, and every "pricing
censored" label at k8+ was an artifact of an unchosen default.

**F4 — Route weight brackets the fleet LP bound (`D0013`).** Bus fixed cost is
exactly 100,000; charging at the LP optimum is $86–$2,469. For the combined-cost
optimum with weight `w` and any minimum-fleet solution, `w - W* <= C/100000 ≈
0.03` buses. So certified route weights are fleet LP bounds to within ~0.03, and
the three-phase lexicographic master became a confirmation rather than a gate.
Uncertified cells still give **no** bound in either direction.

**F5 — Discretization costs whole buses.** Of the 10 certified primary-grid
cells, 4 match GIRO's fleet exactly and 6 require **exactly one more bus**.
Reporting this as "+10.8%" understates it: fleet is integral, and one bus out of
two is 50%.

**F6 — Time discretization binds; SOC saturates (`D0015`).** All three k2 cells
reach route weight **exactly 2.0000, certified, at 1 kWh / 5 min**. Two clean
controlled comparisons at fixed 1.0 kWh SOC, changing only `block_min` 10 -> 5:
`k02_s2` 2.1538 -> 2.0000 and `k02_s3` 2.1400 -> 2.0000. Meanwhile SOC
refinement alone plateaus (`k02_s2`: 2.1875 / 2.1667 / 2.1538 / 2.1538 across
15 / 5 / 2.5 / 1.0 kWh). **This refutes the "GIRO uses 1% SOC steps" explanation**
— 2.5 kWh already is 1% of 300 kWh and does not close the gap. Documented in
`analysis/scale_ladder/ll_20260820c/RESOLUTION_ANSWER_20260820.md`.

**F7 — No algorithm regression at k40.** Historical status files show every
k40 run used the same configuration we do (`singletons` or `artificial`,
15 kWh / 10 min, 300 kWh / 300 kW). The `k40fx` factorial's `PS` arm
(partition + singletons) reached 1249 iterations at its wall limit; we reached
999 in 8 hours, i.e. faster per unit time. The documented "39.x / 39–40 buses"
came from **cover-sense runs on GIRO40-augmented pools** — MIP plumbing, not RAW
discovery, exactly as handoff §4.4 states. **RAW partition CG from singletons has
never solved k40**, and no capability was lost.

**F8 — The primary grid discards 10% of every charge.** 300 kW x 10 min =
50 kWh, floored onto a 15 kWh lattice -> 45 kWh credited. This understates range
and distorts charging cost, which matters directly once prices vary.

**F9 — 32 integer solves completed, zero failures, and are still unread.**

## 4. Physics, now frozen

| parameter | value | basis |
|---|---|---|
| battery | **240 kWh** | source data supports ~239–240 far better than 300 |
| charger power | **240 kW** | 1C, 0->100% in one hour; inside the OppCharge 150/300/450/600 range and conservative for LTO/high-power NMC packs |
| reserve SOC | 0 | operator decision, revisit later |
| charge-start cost | $5, tie-break | also discourages implausible numbers of charge events |
| charger capacity | unlimited | deferred until we have matching solutions |
| deadhead | zone-centroid | accepted simplification; must be stated in methods |
| SOC step | 10 or 2.5 kWh | commensurate: 240 kW x 10 min = 40 kWh, x 5 min = 20 kWh |

Caveat to state, not hide: real charging tapers above ~80% SOC, so constant
power to 100% is mildly optimistic. Standard linear idealization in the E-VSP
literature.

Note the expectation: **240 kWh should make the fleet worse, not better.** Less
range means more charging and more buses. The switch is for fidelity, so that a
future match is credible rather than an artifact of an over-generous battery.
