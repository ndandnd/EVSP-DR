## 2. What is resolved

### Goal 1 — algorithmic trust

**R1. The ladder exists.** Campaign `ll_20260820c`: 96 non-MIP cells submitted,
90+ complete, **zero failures** (`D0011`). Artificials hit zero at iteration 1 in
every cell.

**R2. Pricing is exact and certified.** All nine k2/k3/k5 primary-grid cells
certify (`min_rc = 0`) in 8 s to 86 min. The apparent "first censoring at k8"
was an artefact of `--max-iters` defaulting to 2000, not a runtime wall
(`B0019`) — several cells stopped at 2000 iterations with hours of budget unused.

**R3. The lexicographic phase-2 fleet bound equalled the combined-cost route
weight to 1e-7 on eight k2/k3/k5 cells** *(corrected 2026-08-21: narrowed)*.
The three-phase lexicographic master reports `phase_2_fleet_lp_bound` equal to
the combined-cost route weight to **1e-7** on the eight completed
k2/k3/k5 primary-grid cells, all phases certified (`D0019`). Independently
corroborated: phase-3 charging cost reproduces `lp_obj − 100000 ×
route_weight` to four significant figures (`D0020`). This supersedes the
`D0005` reporting restriction **for those cells**. As a general statement the
identification rests on the `D0013` bracket argument (within ~0.03 buses at
observed charging magnitudes), not a theorem; elsewhere prefer citing the
explicit lexicographic phase-2 bound. **Uncertified** route weights still give
no bound in either direction.

**R4. The fleet gap is decomposed exactly** at `k02_s2` on the primary grid
(`D0023`): industrial fleet 2 → **+1 bus route-space discretization** → true
discretized optimum 3 → **+1 bus column-pool composition** → our pool MIP's 4.
Both terms vanish at 1 kWh / 5 min.

**R5. Time discretization binds; SOC saturates.** Two controlled comparisons at
fixed 1.0 kWh SOC changing only `block_min` 10 → 5: `k02_s2` 2.1538 → 2.0000,
`k02_s3` 2.1400 → 2.0000. SOC refinement alone plateaus (`k02_s2`: 2.1875 /
2.1667 / 2.1538 / 2.1538 across 15 / 5 / 2.5 / 1.0 kWh). **This refutes the
"GIRO uses 1% SOC steps" explanation** — 2.5 kWh already *is* 1% of 300 kWh and
does not close the gap (`D0015`).

**R6. End-to-end RAW recovery achieved and proven.** `k02_s2` and `k02_s3` reach
exactly **2 buses**, proven optimal, from RAW pools at 1 kWh / 5 min with no
injected routes; `k03_s1` and `k03_s3` reach **3** at 1 kWh / 10 min (`D0017`).
Branch-and-price separately **proved** `k02_s2 = 3` on the primary grid and
**= 2** at 1 kWh / 5 min.

**R7. Two independent exact methods agree.** A direct arc-flow MIP returns 3 for
`k02_s2` primary, matching branch-and-price; all three primary k2 cells are
3/3/3 (`D0025`). The arc-flow model enforces **all 36** per-route constraints of
the pricer (`D0030`, closing `B0028`).

**R8. Column generation buys no bound strength here.** Arc-flow LP equals the
set-partitioning LP on all nine primary cells, because a route *is* a
source-to-sink path in the SOC×time DAG and flow decomposition applies
(`D0026`). CG's only justification for this model is tractability at scale.
Consequently the pool-composition failure is **pipeline-induced, not
model-induced** (`D0027`).

**R9. Both cheap pool-side levers are retired.** Diversification saturates after
3 rounds (`B0022`); cross-resolution pool union changes the route space rather
than repairing the pool, and cross-grid costs are not comparable (`D0028`).
`primary_pool target 3 = INFEASIBLE` is a *proof*, not a timeout.

**R10. No k40 regression ever occurred.** Historical k40 runs used the same
configuration we do; the documented "39.x / 39–40 buses" came from cover-sense
runs on GIRO40-**augmented** pools — MIP plumbing, not RAW discovery. RAW
partition CG from singletons has never solved k40.

**R11. Warm pools dramatically improve uncertified k30 endpoints** *(corrected
2026-08-21: they do not "fix k30")*. Route weight 30.79–32.31 against target
30, versus 77–107 with singletons. Five of six warm-pool runs. Those runs
ended **uncertified** (`rc ≈ −100k`, about one bus of headroom), so k30 is not
closed. These endpoints come from the plan-less `warmpool` batch; their
durable evidence is cluster-only `sacct` snapshots (§8) and is not committed
to git.

**R12. `columns_per_iter = 30` is the best tested choice on the matched k20
comparison** *(corrected 2026-08-21: not established as universally optimal)*.
30 beats 100 beats 300 at equal wall time (k20_s1: 20.12 @ 2000 it / 20.73 @
1282 / 21.57 @ 877) — one instance at one scale. 30 stays the default
(`D0100`).

**R13. The resolution crossover is k8.** At or below k5, refinement is
affordable and monotone. At k8 and above, finer time raises the *achievable*
optimum but slows convergence, so within a fixed budget you end up further from
it (k08_s1: 8.59 at (4,2), 12.77 at (2,2), 39.16 at (2,1)).

## 5. Current goals

**Goal 1 is answered at small scale, with proofs.** The open question is no
longer "can we match the industrial fleet" — we can, provably, at 1 kWh / 5 min
and on the event lattice. It is **whether a sufficiently fine representation is
tractable at scale.** That is a legitimate research question with a measurable
answer, and the event lattice is the leading candidate.

**Goal 2 has its first number but it is not yet interpretable.** Three
structural issues must be resolved before any savings figure goes outside the
project: the $5 charge-start term exceeded the entire energy bill (`B0023`); free
initial SOC plus a single-day horizon deletes the overnight trough, which is
where fleet demand response actually lives (`B0024`); and the `peak12` instrument
has a one-hour peak (`B0026`). None is an optimizer deficiency.

## 6. Open questions, ranked

1. **Does fine resolution scale?** `ft_`/`fx_` cells landing — the resolution
   cost surface across 7 grids × 6 scales.
2. **Event lattice at coarse SOC.** If it still certifies 2.0000 at
   `--soc-step 15`, the 9.8 GiB blowup at `k02_s3` disappears and the approach
   scales. SOC saturates below 2.5 kWh, so there is no reason to pair event
   times with 1 kWh SOC.
3. **k40.** Warm pools give 125–136 against target 40, with `rc ≈ −11M` at only
   288–405 iterations in 12 h — **pricing-time-bound at ~107 s/iteration**, a
   different problem from k30.
4. **`B0030`** — capped savings mix "value of re-timing" with "cost of the cap";
   needs a capped-feasible baseline.
5. **A real tariff series** (Nord Pool SE3 for a Partille service day). Without
   it no savings figure is defensible.
6. **`B0029`** — the routing tier permits unlimited charge starts while the
   charging tier caps them; no joint comparison is meaningful until they agree.
7. **Does `run_exact_pool_mip.py` resume from `--progress-dir`, or only publish
   the best incumbent?** If it resumes, nothing needs scaglione and `D0033`
   simplifies.
8. **`B0031`** — six-selection extension used synthetic instances instead of
   GIRO duty unions; correction issued, CSVs not yet committed.
