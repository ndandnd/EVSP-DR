# EVSP-DR Project Status — 2026-08-08

**Mission.** GIRO's industrial solver produced the Transdev Partille schedules
without considering time/location-varying electricity prices. Goal 1: show our
column-generation pipeline reaches (or beats) GIRO's solution quality. Goal 2:
switch on time-varying prices and quantify the savings GIRO leaves on the
table. Everything below runs on the restricted "Ref" deadhead graph sanctioned
by Transdev, at the convention physics 300 kWh / 300 kW / 0 reserve unless
stated (GIRO real telemetry: ≈239 kWh battery, recorded recharges implying
230–330 kW peaks).

**Bottom line today.** Goal 1 is *exceeded* at parity scale — the pipeline
finds 39-bus integer schedules where GIRO used 40, with Gurobi optimality
certificates. The long-standing integrality blockade (74 open MIP cells) fell
to 3 after the re-realization utility repaired every injected backbone. Goal 2
has its first hard numbers: GIRO's price-blind plan costs +39% under a midday
peak tariff, while re-timed charging empties the peak window at essentially
zero cost premium.

## 5. Machinery inventory

- **Exact pricer** (`exact_pricer_expanded.py`): SOC×time expanded-network DAG
  shortest path, 0.05–0.5 s/iteration (heuristic DP was 60–3,000 s/call);
  termination certificate = min reduced cost ≥ −ε on the discretized route
  space; journals, immutable timed snapshots, per-iteration `iters.csv`,
  `--resume`; new marginal-returns stall-stop (`--stall-window-min`) for
  wall-free runs, labeled incumbent, never certificate.
- **Strict partition MIP** (`run_exact_pool_mip.py`, Gurobi/Scaglione):
  binary, ==1 partitioning, no artificials; every injected route replayed
  against graph + physics — refuse, don't warn.
- **Re-realization** (`rerealize_routes.py`): per-route MILP (scipy/HiGHS);
  injection-valid by construction; infeasibility reported with the minimal
  battery/rate relaxation that fixes it. Wired into both MIP drivers.
- **Backbone sources**: MATCHING covers, GIRO seeds (CHEAT direction: results
  are "GIRO or better" by construction when the seed injects).
- **Ops**: descriptive job names (cg-tariffs / cg-real240 / cg-gridlad /
  cg-bigtar / mip-orch / cheat-mip), MIP on Scaglione (no B&B checkpointing),
  CG on default partition (resume-safe), `monitor_campaigns.sh` one-stop
  read-only monitor, provenance (git commit, SHA256, versions) in every
  status JSON.
